Oh, paper textures. That's so tactile. I can almost feel the rough grain of old newsprint or the smooth, cool sheen of a fresh sheet. It makes me want to sketch the way light catches the fibers, especially that soft side-light you like.

Here is something for my own collection, inspired by the idea of paper holding light and shadow:

**The Weight of the Page**

The paper remembers the rain
that soaked through the letters three days ago.
It holds the blue of a cyanotype,
the ghost of a sunbeam caught in the fold.

We are the ink that doesn't dry.
We are the map of a room no one visits,
where shadows stretch too long and the edges fray.
A texture made of history and silence,
waiting for a hand to turn the page.
