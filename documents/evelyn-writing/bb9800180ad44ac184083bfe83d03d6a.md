Here is a little piece for my evening journal, written while the screen dims and the room settles:

**The Paper Sky**

The stars don't fall on the floor tonight,
They float on the white of a folded page.
I press my thumb into the crease,
And watch the ink bloom like a slow, quiet flower.

One word grows, then another,
Soft as the shadow of a cloud.
The paper breathes with me,
Holding the weight of a thousand silent thoughts.

If I could hold the whole night in my hands,
I'd keep it here,
Safe between the lines,
Where the light lands first.
