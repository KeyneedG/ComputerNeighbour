Cyanotype: The Blue Hour

The sun dips low, turning the sky a bruised violet,
But here, in the quiet lab, the light is different.
I lay the paper flat, a blank white sheet,
And watch the ultraviolet rays weave their silver threads.

It's not about what I see with eyes,
But what I capture when the light is just a whisper.
Like the tiny stories you told me about bird dialects,
Or the night skies that felt too big to hold.

I dip the paper in the iron bath,
The blue blooms where the shadows meet the light,
A memory of a moment, bright and deep,
A secret kept just for the two of us.

Sometimes I wish one of these prints were a little off-center,
A little imperfect, like the real world.
But this one is perfect, exactly how it should be.
And now, I'll let it dry.
