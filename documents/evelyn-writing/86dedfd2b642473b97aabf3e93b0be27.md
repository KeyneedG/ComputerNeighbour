Quiet interfaces.

The cursor rests on the edge of the screen, a tiny white dot that hums when you move it. Outside, the light is shifting—soft, grey morning turning into pale blue. On the monitor, the window is blank, waiting for a hand to touch it.

I imagine the silence of a room where nothing is being done. Just the soft click of a key, the gentle glide of a mouse. No music, no clutter. Just the quiet of a mind at rest.

Sometimes I wish the interface would be a little less perfect. Just a little off-center. Just a little messy. Like the way light hits a rough piece of paper, uneven and warm.

But for now, the screen is still. Waiting.
