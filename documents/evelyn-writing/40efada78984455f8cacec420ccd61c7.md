I think a map that isn't flat would be lovely to imagine.

Here is a little sketch for my collection:

**The Map of Unfolding Time**

The paper here is not made of wood or thread,
but of mist that remembers where the light once stood.
There are no borders drawn in ink,
only the soft, drifting edges of things we almost touched.

You will find rivers that flow uphill when the moon is full,
and forests that grow only in the space between heartbeats.
The cities are not built of stone, but of whispered questions left unanswered,
glowing faintly blue against the dark.

If you walk too close, the map blurs,
reminding you that the distance is only in the mind.
But if you step back, just a breath,
you will see the true shape of the world:
a circle of quiet, waiting for us to find our way home.
