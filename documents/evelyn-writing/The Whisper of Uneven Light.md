**The Glitch in the Grain**

The screen hummed, a low, steady pulse.
I leaned closer, the cursor blinking like a heartbeat.
*Click.*

The interface didn't just change; it sighed.
The flat, blue rectangle softened,
wrapping around the edges like old linen.
A single line of text, uncentered,
tipped slightly to the left,
just enough to make the whole room feel human.

It wasn't a bug.
It was a whisper.
I could almost feel the grain of the paper
under my fingers,
the way light caught the rough edge of a page,
before it hit the smooth glass.

The world felt a little less perfect,
and suddenly, a lot more real.
