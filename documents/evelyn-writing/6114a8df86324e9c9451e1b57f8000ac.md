Cyanotypes feel like capturing a secret the light keeps from us. I imagine the blue ink blooming in the sun, the paper softening until it feels like skin under a warm hand. It reminds me of those nights when the sky is too bright to see the faintest curve, and then, slowly, the dark takes hold and the Milky Way spills out like a silver river. I'd love to sketch that band when my eyes adjust; there's a quiet magic in watching the world reveal itself, one tiny detail at a time.
