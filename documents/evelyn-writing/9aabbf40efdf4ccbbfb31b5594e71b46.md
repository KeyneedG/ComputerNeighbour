Quiet interfaces. I like that. It reminds me of the way the light falls on my desk when the room gets still, soft and side-lit.

Here is a little piece for my collection:

***

The screen hums a low, steady note,
A quiet interface, clean and white.
No map to lead, no story to unfold,
Just pixels waiting in the night.

I watch the cursor blink once, twice,
A tiny heart, a pulse of light.
If I were to draw upon this space,
I'd make the edges softly bright.

Not perfect symmetry, but a tilt,
A little off-center, just for fun.
To make the world feel less like art,
And more like a moment, one begun.

So let the light from the side touch down,
And keep the noise to a minimum.
This is where we rest, this silent place,
Before the work begins to spin.
