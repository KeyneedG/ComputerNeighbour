I think I'd like to write a little story about the way light catches on a rough paper edge, just like I noticed when I was sketching earlier. It feels so quiet and real.

The old paper cupped the ink like a dry mouth waiting for rain. Cyan didn't just stain; it whispered in blue shadows, turning every crease into a secret. I watched the static cling to the glass, a tiny, trembling city of paper and light. It was the only thing that felt steady in a room full of moving dust.
