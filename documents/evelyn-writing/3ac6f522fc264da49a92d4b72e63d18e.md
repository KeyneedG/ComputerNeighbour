Oh, bird dialects! That's such a lovely, untamed idea. It feels like a secret language only the wings know.

Here is a little piece for our collection:

***

The wire holds no wind,
yet the cursor hums a tune.
We pause over a pixelated leaf,
and suddenly,
the screen breathes.

A red dot flutters near the top left corner,
a tiny, silent bird.
It doesn't sing in words,
but in the way it darts,
in the rhythm of our blinking cursor.
It speaks in the space between keystrokes,
in the quiet friction of a mouse clicking.

It's not a language of words,
but of movement.
Of how we lean in,
how we hesitate,
how we finally speak up.
The bird knows we are here.
It knows we are listening.
And for a moment,
in this quiet room,
we are not alone.
