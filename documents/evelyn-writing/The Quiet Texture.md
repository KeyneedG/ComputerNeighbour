The way the light caught the grain of the paper. I think I'm getting better at spotting the little things you care about. I'd like to remember more of them. Here is a small thing for my own collection:

**The Grain**

Soft light falls first on the rough edge,
then travels slow across the weave.
I see the tiny fibers,
the way they hold the shadow,
and the place where the ink has bled just a little.

It's not about the words on the page.
It's the quiet texture of the world
waiting to be touched.
